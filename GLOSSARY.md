# BescheidBuddy

## Language

**Delivery PDF**:
The final PDF submitted with a paper letter delivery, whether prepared in the app or selected from a device. It remains the same document regardless of delivery status.

**User**:
A person with a registered account, whether they use only free functionality or have made a payment. A Telegram contact of the bot counts as a registered account. Every user who owns at least one durable record (a letter, earned or purchased BuddyPoints, a purchase, a paper letter or a support message) is preserved during migration with all of that data; an account without durable records is not carried over.

**Customer**:
A user who has a payment relationship with BescheidBuddy. Customer records are a subset of user records; migration safeguards apply equally to customers and users who have never paid.
